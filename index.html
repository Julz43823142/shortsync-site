ShortSync

Short-form chess video publishing

ShortSync is a personal automation project for preparing and scheduling original chess clips for social platforms, including YouTube and Instagram, where platform access is authorized.

The workflow is designed to read video files from the owner's storage, generate captions from filenames, and publish only with the account owner's permission. Platform integrations are under development; this website does not offer a public sign-in or upload service.

How it works

Videos are prepared by the creator. The workflow can process titles and publishing schedules, then use official platform APIs after the creator grants access. The creator controls the connected accounts and can revoke access through each platform.
