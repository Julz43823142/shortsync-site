ShortSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

ShortSync is a personal video-publishing automation project. It is not currently a public service and does not offer public user accounts.

Information processed

If authorized by the account owner, the automation may process video files, filenames, captions, publishing schedules, platform account identifiers, and the OAuth permissions needed to upload videos. Access credentials must be stored privately, not in the public website or repository.

Purpose and sharing

This information is used to prepare and publish the account owner's videos through the selected platform APIs. Video content and captions are sent to the selected platform when publishing is enabled. ShortSync does not sell personal information.

Storage and retention

The public website does not include analytics scripts or a visitor registration form. GitHub Pages, which hosts the website, may process technical request information under GitHub's own privacy policy. Automation data may be processed by the storage and automation providers chosen by the owner. The owner is responsible for controlling retention and removing stored credentials or logs when no longer needed.

Revoking access

Connected platform permissions can be revoked in the relevant Google or Meta account security settings. The owner can also remove stored automation credentials and stop the workflow.

Contact

For questions, use the ShortSync repository issue tracker. Do not post passwords, tokens, or personal data in public issues.
